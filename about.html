About Me



I am a second-year Computer Information Technology student at Valencia College, fascinated by networking and cloud-based applications. I have always loved computers and the internet as a concept. When I was younger, it seemed almost like magic, typing away on a keyboard only for the letters to magically appear on the screen in front of me. Now, I know the inner workings of that keyboard, screen, and the computer that connects them, as well as the vast web of networks that connect the computer to the rest of the internet.


I am a creative person who loves finding solutions to difficult problems, such as missing IP information, which I have encountered while working on a network of virtual Windows servers. My aim for the future is to use creativity and problem-solving skills to build, improve, and customize cloud-based applications as a cloud developer.
